Mahjong Efficiency Privacy policy

Effective: August 1, 2026

Information we collect

This app sends a randomly issued anonymous player ID, your rating, the problems you answered, the tiles you chose, whether they matched, your answer times and the positions you analyzed to our server. We do not collect your name, address, email address, advertising ID or location. For information handled by advertising, see "Advertising".When you create a transfer code, we store a one-way digest of it and its expiry on our server for 30 minutes. It is removed once the code is used or expires. The code itself is never stored.

How we use it

We use it only to deliver problems, grade answers, compute ratings, update answer statistics, run the efficiency analyzer, prevent abuse and improve service quality. We do not use it for tracking or sell it to third parties.

Advertising

This app shows ads through Google AdMob, an ad service of Google LLC. To serve ads, Google may receive device information such as IP address, device type, OS version and how ads were shown or tapped. This app does not use personalized ads and does not ask for permission to track you across apps. For how Google handles information, see Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

About posts

In Everyone's Nanikiru you can post mahjong positions. The note you attach reaches only the operator and is not shown to other users. Inappropriate posts such as advertising, harassment or content unrelated to mahjong may be removed. You can post up to five times a day. For inquiries, use the email address on the support page.

Information on your device

Your player name, saved hand records and local comments stay on your device. They are not sent to the server unless a feature that you explicitly use sends them.

Retention and deletion

Anonymous player data on the server is kept until you delete it from the settings screen. Running "Delete all data" removes the anonymous ID, rating and answer history on the server together with the player data and saved hand records on this device.

Security

Communication with production uses HTTPS, and administration features are separated from the public app. We do not collect personally identifying information beyond what the service needs.

Changes

If we change what we collect or why, we will update this screen and the privacy information on the App Store.
