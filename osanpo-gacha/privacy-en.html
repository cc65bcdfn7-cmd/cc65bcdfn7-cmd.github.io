Detour Today Privacy Policy

Effective: November 6, 2026
Operator: Mebae studio
Detour Today is called 「きょうの寄り道」 in Japanese.

1. Information we collect

The operator of Detour Today (the "App") does not collect your personal information. The App has no operator servers, no account registration, and no analytics added by the operator (for what the advertising SDK collects for ads, see section 3). Ads are provided by Google AdMob (section 3). The App does not ask for permission to use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

2. Information used on your device

  • Current location: Used only (1) when you choose "Current location" as the starting point and tap "Pick a spot" or "Create course", to set the starting point, and (2) when you tap the current-location button on the result map. Location permission is "While Using the App". Your location is never sent to the operator or stored.
  • Settings: Settings such as travel mode, time and place types are saved on your device for the next time you open the App.
  • Ad records: The date and time you started using the App and the time an ad was last shown are saved on your device, so that no ads are shown for the first 7 days and for 30 minutes after an ad. They are not sent to the operator.
  • Saved courses: Courses you save are stored only on your device: the course name (editable), saved date and time, travel mode, whether it returns to the start, start and end times, distance and travel time, and for each stop its name, type, coordinates and arrival/departure times, plus the label of the starting point ("Current location" or the station/address text you entered). The coordinates of the starting point are not saved. You can delete them in the App's list; they are removed when you delete the App.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following services. Data is handled under each provider's privacy policy.

  • Apple Maps (MapKit): Used to show maps, search for places, calculate routes and show place details. The search area, route start and destination coordinates, the map area shown (around your location when you tap the current-location button), and any station/address text you type are sent to Apple. Tapping a stop or "Open in Maps" opens the route in the Apple Maps app. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (ads): From the 8th day of use, a full-screen ad (image or video, closable within seconds) may be shown before a spot or course is displayed; closing it shows the result. At most one ad every 30 minutes. From day 8, ads are pre-loaded, for example when you open the App (not during the first 7 days). Ads are requested as non-personalized ads, and the setting that lets the ad SDK use your device location is turned off. Even so, to serve and measure ads, Google collects device and app information when ads are loaded and shown (such as IP address and the approximate region derived from it, device identifiers, ads viewed and interactions, app performance and crash data) and uses it for ad delivery, measurement and analytics under Google's policies. How Google uses information from ads · Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps: Only when you open a "View in Google Maps" link or a shared course link, Safari or the Google Maps app opens. The link contains the place name and area or the coordinates of the stop; the starting point is never included (route links start from the first stop). Google Privacy Policy

4. Sharing a course

When you tap "Share", the course text (date and times, travel mode, stop types and names, Google Maps links for each stop and a route link through the stops) can be sent with an app you choose. The starting point is not included. The receiving app's policies apply.

5. Children

The App can be used by people of all ages. The operator does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are handled as described in section 3, and only ads suitable for general audiences are requested.

6. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page. If the Japanese version and this English version differ, the Japanese version prevails.
