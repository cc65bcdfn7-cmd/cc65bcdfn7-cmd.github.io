Detour Today Support

An iPhone app that picks a walk or drive destination, or a date course, at random — just enter a starting point and how much time you have.

Contact

Please send bug reports and feedback to: mebaegames@gmail.com

It helps if you include your iPhone model, iOS version, and which screen you were on and what you did.

FAQ

"No places found" appears

  • Make the time or distance a little longer, or select more place types.
  • Tapping "Again" many times in a row can hit the map search limit temporarily. Please wait a moment and try again.

About ads

  • No ads for the first 7 days. From day 8, a full-screen ad may appear before a spot or course is shown; close it to see the result. At most one ad every 30 minutes, and "Again" during that time shows no ad.
  • If an ad can't be loaded (for example, with a weak connection), the result is shown without an ad.
  • If you see an inappropriate ad or one not suitable for your age, please let us know at the email above (the date and time and what the ad showed help).

I don't want to allow location access

In "Starting point" on the settings screen, choose to enter a place and type a station name or address. The app works without your current location.

Deleting a saved course

Tap the heart icon (saved courses) at the top right of the first screen to open the list, swipe left on the course and tap "Delete".

Opening hours

Long-press a stop (or use the card's button in single-spot mode) and open the place details or "View in Google Maps" to check opening hours. Please check before you go.

Does a shared course include my home location?

No. Shared text contains the date and times, travel mode, stop types and names, and Google Maps links for the stops. The route link starts from the first stop; the starting point is never included.
