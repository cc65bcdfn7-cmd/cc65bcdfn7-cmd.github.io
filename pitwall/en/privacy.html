PitWall Privacy Policy

Effective date / last updated: November 28, 2026

Operator: Mebae Games

PitWall stores and uses game data such as setups, garage cars, race records, and personal bests on your device. It has no app account registration, built-in cloud sync, or online multiplayer. This version does not authenticate with Game Center or submit times to it.

This version has no ads, in-app purchases, or developer-operated gameplay analytics or crash-report uploads. It has no feature that sends game data to a developer server. We do not track you across other apps or websites for advertising.

Local data is used to retain settings and records across launches. Deleting the app may remove local saves. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or restore your local saves through the app. Apple services such as OS backups follow Apple's settings and policies.

Support and privacy web pages open in your device's browser.

If you contact support, the contact details and problem description you choose to provide will be used to respond. Please do not send secrets or unnecessary personal information.

Privacy contact: mebaegames@gmail.com (support page)

Changes will be announced on this page with an updated effective date.
