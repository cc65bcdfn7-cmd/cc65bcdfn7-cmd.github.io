PitWall Support

Operator: Mebae Games / Last updated: November 28, 2026

PitWall is a racing simulation where you tune the car and its strategy, watch it run, and compare results. It is free, with no ads or in-app purchases.

Contact

Email: mebaegames@gmail.com

For a problem report, include the app version and build number, device model, OS version, steps taken, and what happened. Hide personal information in screenshots. Do not send passwords, certificates, or a complete copy of device save files.

Getting started

Select a strategy, open Tune, change values, and choose Confirm. Run solo, open the replay from the results, then adjust and try again. Local races use your saved past car setups and AI opponents. For 10–20 lap pit races, set tires, fuel, and pit conditions before the start. The car pits automatically when the planned lap or tire-wear condition is met.

No sound

Check the device volume and audio output, then play a replay at normal speed. Audio stops while paused and during fast-forward above 2×.

Saving

Confirmed setups, garage cars, race records, and personal bests are handled on your device. The app has no built-in cloud sync or transfer between devices. Deleting or reinstalling the app may remove saved data; please do not use this as the first troubleshooting step.

Other

You can switch between Japanese and English on screen. Use the app in landscape orientation. This version does not use Game Center.
