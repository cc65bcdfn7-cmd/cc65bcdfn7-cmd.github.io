Koe Recipe Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This page explains how Koe Recipe (the "App") handles your information. The Japanese version of this policy (https://cc65bcdfn7-cmd.github.io/koerecipe/privacy.html) is the original. This English text has the same meaning.

1. Summary

  • There is no separate sign-up. Posting, liking and reporting require you to be signed in to iCloud. These community features are available only in the Japanese version of the App.
  • Public posts, likes and reports are sent to Apple's CloudKit. Your voice, menus and shopping lists are never sent.
  • All cooking and menu features are free. Ads (Google AdMob) appear on the Home and Menu Roulette screens.
  • If you buy the monthly auto-renewing subscription "Ad-Free", ads stop appearing.
  • No ads are shown while you cook (during voice control, reading aloud, timers and confirmations), or on the recipe, weekly menu and shopping list screens.
  • Before showing ads, the App may show the iPhone's tracking permission prompt (App Tracking Transparency) once. It does not appear if your iPhone is set not to allow such requests or if you have already answered. Every feature works whether or not you allow tracking. Ads can be tailored to you only if you allow it (and, in regions where a consent screen is shown, depending on that consent).

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following on your iPhone. If you use iPhone backups (iCloud or computer backups), this data may be included in them.

  • Your weekly menu and shopping checks
  • Your place in a recipe while cooking (so you can resume)
  • Your favorite recipes
  • The types of dishes you pick for Menu Roulette (main, side, soup)

This is not sent to the developer's servers. If you delete the App, the data on your device is deleted too.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

During cooking, the App asks for microphone and speech recognition permission so you can move through steps with voice commands such as "next" or "repeat".

  • Your voice is turned into text only inside your iPhone (on-device speech recognition). Neither your voice nor the text is sent outside your device. This applies to every language the App supports, including English.
  • If on-device speech recognition is not available on your iPhone for the language you are using, voice control is not available. The App does not switch to server-based recognition, and does not switch to another language's recognition.
  • Voice recordings are not saved.
  • You can change the permissions anytime in the iPhone's Settings. Without them, you can still use recipes with on-screen controls.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

  • To show ads, the App uses Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent management.
  • For ad delivery, measurement and SDK improvement, Google may receive device and app identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, and performance and diagnostic information. For how Google handles this, see Google's Privacy Policy and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services".
  • Tracking permission (ATT): after consent (UMP) and before the first ad request, the App may show the iPhone's permission prompt once (it will not appear, depending on your iPhone settings or earlier answers). It is not shown while you cook or while you subscribe to "Ad-Free". The App's message reads: "To show ads tailored to you and measure their effect, we link your advertising identifier with usage data from other companies' apps and sites. Every feature works even if you do not allow this."

- If you allow it: the App uses your advertising identifier (IDFA) and requests ads tailored to you (within the scope of your consent, in regions where a consent screen is shown). Google may link the advertising identifier with information from other companies' apps and sites to show ads and measure their effect.

- If you do not allow it, or it is restricted: the App requests only ads that are not tailored to you (non-personalized ads). It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

- To change it later: in the iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, switch this App on or off. When you return to the App, the change applies from the next ad.

  • Ad content is limited to all ages. The App never asks for your location. Ad sound is turned off.
  • The contents of your voice, menus, shopping lists and favorites are not given to ads.
  • Ads are not served in the EU (27 countries). In the UK, Switzerland and some other places, a consent screen (UMP) appears before ads. The App's features work even if you do not consent. You can change your consent from "Ad settings" in the App's Settings.
  • While you subscribe to "Ad-Free", the App does not show ads and does not request them.
  • If you buy "Ad-Free" after ads have started, the ad SDK may stay loaded until you restart the App. Even then, the App does not show ads and does not request them.

5. In-app purchase ("Ad-Free")

  • Apple handles purchase, renewal, cancellation and refunds. Neither the App nor the developer receives your payment details.
  • The App asks Apple (StoreKit) every time to find out whether you are subscribed. Your purchase status is never sent to the developer's servers.
  • You can cancel in the iPhone's Settings > your Apple Account > Subscriptions.

6. Analytics and tracking

Posts, likes and reports are handled only to provide those features, and the App does not analyze your usage except for ads. What Google's ad SDK collects and how tracking permission is handled are described in "4. Ads" above. If you do not allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.

7. Children

The App handles a nickname when you post. Do not put contact details or personal information in a post. What Google's ad SDK receives is described in "4. Ads". Ad content is limited to all ages.

Posts (Japanese version only)

Posting, browsing and liking community recipes ("Minna no Recipe") is available only when the App is used in Japanese in version 1.3. The following applies to people who use these features in the Japanese version. It is included here for completeness.

  • Published recipes (dish name, ingredients, amounts, steps, type, time, servings), photos and nicknames, and likes and reports, are stored in Apple's CloudKit. They are linked to your CloudKit user record ID and creation time. The purpose is the App's own features: publishing posts, showing likes and responding to inappropriate content. Post content is not used for ads or tracking.
  • Posts are published after operator review. After approval, the text, photo, nickname and post time are shown to the public. Content that is in review or rejected is also stored in the CloudKit public database and may be readable from outside the App. Reports are not made public; they are handled by the reporter and the operator. The operator reviews reports and can hide posts and authors that violate the rules.
  • For photos, the App receives only the one you pick with PhotosPicker, removes original metadata such as location before publishing, and converts it to a JPEG of 500 KB or less. It cannot remove what appears in the photo, or locations or personal information you write in the text yourself.
  • What you write and the original photo are kept on your device whether publishing succeeds or fails. iPhone backups may apply to drafts and originals. Blocks, hidden reports of your own and the mark of agreeing to the terms are also kept on your device.
  • You can remove your own post from public view in the App. Removing it does not delete the original on your device. Deleting the App also deletes the originals on your device. Posts already published are not removed just by deleting the App, so delete the post first or ask support. The operator cannot delete copies that others have saved.
  • You can report text or photos and block authors from the recipe screen. Reports that have not been sent are kept on your device and resent after you reconnect. For deletion requests and inquiries, contact support. The operator deletes report records that are no longer needed.

On the operator's devices only, notification permission is requested the first time the review screen is used. Notifications about posts waiting for review include only the post title, not photos or text.
