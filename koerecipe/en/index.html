Frequently asked questions

Voice control does not respond.

Check in the iPhone's Settings that this App is allowed to use the microphone and speech recognition. A loud kitchen can make it hard to pick up your voice. Keeping your iPhone close helps. Use short phrases such as "next" or "repeat".

I see a message that voice control is not available.

This App recognizes your voice only inside your iPhone. If on-device recognition is not available for the language you are using on your iPhone, voice control is not available. You can still use the on-screen buttons.

My menu or favorites disappeared.

They are stored on your iPhone (they may be included in iPhone backups). They are deleted if you delete the App. The App has no transfer feature of its own (if you restore from an iPhone backup, your data may come back).

Does it cost anything?

All cooking and menu features are free. Ads appear on the Home and Menu Roulette screens. No ads are shown while you cook (during voice control, reading aloud and timers), or on the recipe, weekly menu and shopping list screens.

Can I remove ads?

Buy the monthly auto-renewing subscription "Ad-Free" and ads stop appearing. You can buy it from the gear icon (Settings) at the top right of Home. The price is shown on the purchase screen. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period.

I changed iPhones or reinstalled the App.

Tap "Restore purchases" on the Settings screen. If you bought with the same Apple Account, Ad-Free is restored.

How do I cancel?

Go to the iPhone's Settings > your Apple Account > Subscriptions, or use "Manage subscription" on the App's Settings screen.

An ad consent screen appeared.

Ads are not served in the EU (27 countries). In the UK, Switzerland and some other places, Google's consent screen appears before ads. The App's features work whichever you choose. You can change your consent from "Ad settings" in Settings.

Why can't I see community recipes in English?

In this version, posting and browsing community recipes ("Minna no Recipe") are available only when the App is used in Japanese. The 13 built-in recipes, menus and shopping lists work in English.

Requirements

iPhone (iOS 17.0 or later). Portrait orientation.

Reporting and deleting posts (Japanese version)

Community posts are shown only in the Japanese version. To report inappropriate text or photos, choose "Report" on the recipe screen and pick a reason. The post is hidden on your device right away. If you cannot connect, the report is kept unsent and resent after you reconnect. "Block author" hides that person's posts. You can undo it under "Blocked people" in Settings.

To contact the operator, request deletion or file an objection: mebaegames@gmail.com

Please tell us the dish name, the author name, the post date and what the problem is. Do not send personal or confidential information. The operator aims to review and respond within 24 hours (this is not an Apple deadline).

You can open and delete your own published posts from Settings > "My posts". Originals and drafts stay on your device. The 13 built-in recipes work without iCloud and without a connection. Loading posts and showing likes depend on your connection.
