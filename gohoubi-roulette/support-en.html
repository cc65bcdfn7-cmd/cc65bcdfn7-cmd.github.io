Getting started

On first launch, set a parent PIN. Tap the lock at the top right of the home screen to open the parent menu, then add profiles, tasks and prizes.

Tickets and prizes

Give tickets and check balances under "Child profiles". Check prize stock and dates under "Prizes". If a spin is not possible, check that there are tickets left and that at least one prize is active, in stock and within budget.

Backing up records

Use "Backup" in the parent menu to export and restore your records. To restore, paste the exported text. After a restore, the PIN on the current device stays in use.
