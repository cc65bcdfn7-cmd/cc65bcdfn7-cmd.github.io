Privacy Policy

This policy covers Gohoubi Roulette for iPhone. Effective date: November 21, 2026.

Your records

Nicknames, tasks, prizes, points, spins and other records you enter are processed and stored only on your device. The app does not send any records to the developer. There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party services. The app does not connect to the internet.

The parent PIN is not stored as-is; only a salted hash used for checking it is stored on the device. A copy made before a restore, and any unreadable save file set aside by the app, may also remain on the device. You can use a nickname instead of a real name for each profile.

Device backups and manual export

Depending on your iCloud or device backup settings, your records may be included in your own backups. The developer cannot access your backups.

In the parent menu, "Backup" → "Export & copy" copies your records to the clipboard. The exported text does not include the PIN or its hash. Where you paste or keep it is up to you. Other apps on the device may be able to read the clipboard.

Deleting records

Archiving a profile keeps its past records. To delete all records on the device, delete the app. Restoring the device or app from a backup may bring records back; manage backups and exported copies where you keep them.

Children

The app is designed to be set up and managed by a parent. Children's nicknames are entered by the parent and are never sent anywhere by the app.

Contact

Please use the email address on the support page. We use your email address and message only to reply and to investigate problems. To have your message deleted, let us know at the same address.
