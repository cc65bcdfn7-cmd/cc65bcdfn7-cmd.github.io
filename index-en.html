FAQ

My rating disappeared after changing phones
When moving from iPhone to iPhone, your data transfers automatically if iCloud Keychain is on. If it did not, or if you are moving to or from Android, open Settings on the old device, choose "Move to another device" to show a code, and enter it under "Enter a transfer code" in Settings on the new device. A code is valid for 30 minutes and can be used once. After transferring, the old device can no longer play. If you no longer have the old device, your rating and history cannot be restored.

I want to delete my data
Use "Delete all data" in Settings to delete the anonymous player data on the server (anonymous ID, rating, answer history) and the player data and saved game records on your device.

I cannot start a Rate challenge
Rate challenge is free up to 3 sets per day. After that, watching one ad adds one more set. The count resets to 3 each day. An unfinished set can always be resumed.

"Cannot connect to the server" appears
Rate challenge and Efficiency analyzer need a network connection. Please try again where you have a signal.

I cannot post (5 per day)
